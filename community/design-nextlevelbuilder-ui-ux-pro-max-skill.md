# nextlevelbuilder/ui-ux-pro-max-skill

<https://github.com/nextlevelbuilder/ui-ux-pro-max-skill> · Python · MIT · fit 🟡

Design skill with a "design system generator": from a short project brief
it picks page pattern, style, colors, fonts, effects, things to avoid and a
pre-delivery checklist (contrast, focus states, reduced motion, sizes from
375 to 1440 px). Has a Vietnamese README.

## When we would use it

- Quick landing page or marketing site with a ready look.
- Its pre-delivery checklist is good on its own.

## Watch-outs

- Overlaps Impeccable: pick one, not both.
- Leans toward marketing pages, less toward tools and dashboards.
- Last release 2026-08 (repo still active).
